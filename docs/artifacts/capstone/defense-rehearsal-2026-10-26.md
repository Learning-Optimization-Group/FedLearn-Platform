## Questions to practice

1. **What is original here?** An auditable implementation/integration and scoped empirical findings, not a new DP theorem or universal reducer.
2. **Why does this count as computing work?** Trace the real API/worker/coordinator contracts, per-client state/RNG, transport qualification and failure semantics. Do not claim an unimplemented hospital deployment.
3. **Are the two confidence intervals interchangeable?** No; they condition on different sources of uncertainty and neither is joint uncertainty.
4. **Does a client privacy unit protect an individual patient?** Under fixed preprocessing and assignment, one honest patient's change is contained within a client replacement, but the end-to-end pipeline and any stronger patient guarantee remain unqualified.
5. **Did projection defeat a mismatched attacker?** It was given matching projected features/head; the uniform-mean surrogate remains a genuine attacker limitation.
6. **Why did centered clipping fail privately?** Report the measurements. Stale noisy history is a hypothesis, not an established causal explanation.
7. **Why are three hosts slower?** The result includes cold-start/staging and uncontrolled host differences. No causal bottleneck was isolated; do not claim polling alone explains it.
8. **Why no independent cohort?** Approved access was unavailable. The pre-agreed access gate failed and research stopped; the limitation is explicit.
9. **What does clean reproduction prove?** The bounded CPU profile runs without inherited packages and from tracked source. It does not certify every platform feature or clinical security.
10. **What would you do with more time?** A genuinely independent cohort under a prespecified protocol. Other extensions require their own accepted scope.
