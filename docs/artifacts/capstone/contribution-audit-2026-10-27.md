# Capstone contribution audit

27 October 2026 · Scope: frozen report v5 and retained public evidence.

**Verdict:** The work establishes an engineering artifact and workload-specific empirical results. It does not establish algorithmic novelty, state-of-the-art performance, or a previously unknown general phenomenon. This is a targeted contribution audit, not an exhaustive literature review or a determination of degree eligibility.

**What prior work already establishes.** Privacy–robustness–utility tension is an existing research problem: [Allouah et al., ICML 2023](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/allouah23a.html) provide bounds and SAFE-DSHB. [DP-BREM, USENIX Security 2025](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity25/presentation/gu-xiaolan) combines privacy and Byzantine robustness using client momentum, with a separate secure protocol. Random projection in private optimization also predates this project; [D2P2-SGD, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09485) combines projection, clipping and private SGD. These have different mechanisms and assumptions; they defeat broad originality claims, not constitute matched experimental controls. [ByzFL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.24802) already supports configurable robust-FL benchmarking. [NVIDIA FLARE](https://nvflare.readthedocs.io/en/2.9.0/user_guide/admin_guide/security/differential_privacy.html) already provides privacy filters and Opacus integration. Neither benchmarking nor combining privacy with FL is a new contribution here.

**The precise question this work answers:** Under the declared fixed-feature NIH Effusion recipe, trusted-server client-replacement mechanism and owned-data PGD heuristic, how do four composite policies trade clean and attacked AUROC at matched nominal privacy budgets? Separately, can the canonical CPU training path reproduce numerical results across three heterogeneous hosts, and what execution cost does that placement incur?

**What prevents a stronger research claim.** Policies were selected using repeatedly examined patients; new seeds do not undo that selection. The frozen linear head, simulated IID clients, one dataset/task and mean-surrogate attacker restrict generality. Projection changes representation, clipping and optimization together, so reduced noise dimension is not an isolated causal explanation. SAFE-DSHB, DP-BREM and FLARE were not reproduced as matched baselines. The full research transcript is not a qualified private release. Test counts measure verification effort, not originality. The exact configuration being different does not itself establish a research gap.

**Defensible contribution statement:** “We implement and qualify an inspectable federated-learning experiment path, measure privacy/aggregation trade-offs on a fixed chest-X-ray feature workload, and quantify numerical agreement and execution cost across three heterogeneous CPU hosts.”

**Decision:** Present the existing work as an engineering capstone with bounded empirical findings. Obtain advisor agreement that this satisfies the actual rubric before adding scope. If methodological novelty is required, the current evidence does not meet that requirement; a precise literature-supported gap and evidence addressing it would be needed. External validation would strengthen validity without automatically creating novelty. Keep the research freeze. The proposed failure drill can improve reliability evidence, but cannot repair an originality deficit.
