## Decisions supported by the fixed study

The primary interval is conditional on fixed cohorts and ten simulation seeds.
Head/cohort/clipping results use three matched seeds and are descriptive. None is
a production privacy-budget choice, clinical recommendation or universal rule ranking.

| Decision | Measured evidence | Concrete platform action | Applicability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Choose aggregation from absolute utility and its control | At C0.05 / epsilon 4/sign-flip, mean AUROC 0.627389 versus trimming 0.618250. The primary interaction is nevertheless +0.002266, with paired 95% CI [+0.000704,+0.003828]. | Show clean/attacked absolute endpoints and each matched no-DP control beside the interaction and rule-specific sensitivity/noise. | Ten-seed linear-head primary setting; this does not establish a universal winner. |
| Qualify clipping jointly with attacks and DP | At C0.4/sign-flip, no-DP mean is 0.495854 versus trimming 0.658057; epsilon 4 values are 0.520096 and 0.546027. | Validate clipping with the intended threat/privacy recipe. Preserve the frozen study; new tuning belongs on validation in a separate experiment. | Three seeds; clipping changes useful signal, permitted attack magnitude and noise together. |
| Demand a learning benefit before increasing the head | Clean epsilon 4 mean: linear 0.644213 versus MLP512 0.643233; modeled upload 6.8 MB versus 1,688.0688 MB across 80 clients/20 rounds. | Put matched learning controls and payload estimates into head/model admission and comparison views. | About 248.245 times the payload; no equivalence test or claim that a differently tuned larger head cannot improve. |
| Qualify learning as cohort size changes | Clean epsilon 4 mean: 10 clients 0.548356, 80 clients 0.644213, 1,000 clients 0.630644. The thousand-client private/no-DP gap is smaller, but its private point mean is lower than at 80. | Report accepted cohort and local training recipe together; requalify local steps before scaling client count. | Synthetic patient groups and three seeds; not physical-device scaling or a pure 1/n noise experiment. |
| Treat threshold transfer as a measured operating point | For clean epsilon 4 mean, validation 80% recall transfers to test recall 0.915729 and specificity 0.241375; default test recall is 0.619790. | Display the validation target, transferred threshold and actual held-out confusion counts/recall/specificity. | Ten-model mean; no demonstrated clinical threshold or guarantee of 80% recall on new data. |
| Keep the evaluation unit visible | For that same group, inverse-patient weighting raises AUROC by 0.077657 on average, from 0.646336 to 0.723993. | Retain the original image endpoint and show patient-weight sensitivity alongside it. | A change of weighting, not a patient classifier; do not replace the prespecified endpoint after seeing the higher value. |
| Preserve the content-audit boundary | Removing the nine prespecified ambiguous images changes any model's AUROC by at most 0.000165502. | Keep the official primary cohort and report the fixed secondary mask, rather than remove inconvenient test cases. | Small measured sensitivity does not certify all labels, near-duplicates or clinical validity. |
| Expose actual execution and resource scopes | All 360 cells/7,200 rounds qualified; child 10446.316 s plus parent 2834.088 s. Maximum engine-completion high-water RSS 1573.469 MiB. | Show lifecycle, stage timings, accepted counts, completion/failure and measured-versus-modeled fields explicitly. | No process-tree/late-artifact memory cap or isolated hardware runtime comparison; power/background/fixed-order limitations apply. |

All comparisons refer to the pinned frozen-feature NIH workload. The mobile
pneumonia task and production image-training recipes require their own qualification.
The Simulation Lab should expose these comparisons and limitations. Its persistent
API passed the execution gate at that historical checkpoint; later bounded browser evidence
is tracked in the current checklist. The [follow-up readiness audit](privacy-security-readiness-2026-10-20.md)
separates central DP, generic local DP and DeComFL secure aggregation, with the
remaining qualification/reporting gates. It supplies no new disease-model result.

## Decisions supported by the Effusion follow-up (22 October)

All evidence below comes from the pre-registered
[Effusion protocol](effusion-baseline-protocol-v1.md) and its amendments A1–A10. The
setting is R0 frozen ImageNet features, a linear head, 80 whole-patient IID simulated
clients and client-level DP, evaluated on the selection half unless marked "holdout".
Platform changes are cited by their local commit; none are pushed.

| Decision | Measured evidence | Platform action | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A privacy flag must never be silently ignored | Before `5fad502`, a DP-enabled or regulated project on FedAvg, FedProx, FedOpt, Robust or DeComFL trained **without DP** and registered an artifact the gate accepted. Secure aggregation off DeComFL logged "ENABLED" while updates went in plaintext. | **Implemented locally (`5fad502`):** backend spawn seam and `fl_server.py` refuse DP on strategies without it and secure aggregation off DeComFL. Tests encode the refusal. | Reached through the API (`dpEnabled`/`regulated`); the dashboard exposes no DP control. Merge to the shipping branch is the user's decision. |
| FedAvg projects need real central DP | `DPFedAvg` reuses FedLoRA's mechanism and accountant. It is verified through the production coordinator and safetensors wire, and z = 0 on equal shards reproduces FedAvg exactly. | **Implemented locally (`969755a`):** `--strategy FedAvg --dp-enabled` builds `DPFedAvg`, with the eval-card DP trace and FedLoRA's live-accounting refusals. | Uniform (not example-weighted) averaging under DP. No physical-device run yet. |
| Plan ε before a run | The RMS Gaussian-noise norm divided by the maximum clipped-mean signal norm is: z√d/N for add/remove noise zC/N (the planner and production `dp_aggregate`), 2z√d/N for replace-one 2C/N (the capstone simulator). At ε 4, d = 1,026 and N = 80 that is about 2.3 or 4.6 respectively (corrected 22 October; `plan_central_dp(adjacency="replace_one")` now gives the second figure). Head width cost: the 2× wider R1 head lost more AUROC at every ε (+0.007/+0.017/+0.051 at ε 8/4/1, all intervals > 0; [A4](effusion-privacy-secure-v1-result-2026-10-22.md)). | **Implemented locally (`969755a`):** `fedlearn.privacy.planner.plan_central_dp`. Prefer narrow trainable heads (frozen-head arms) for DP projects. | Formula-exact; the utility mapping comes from one workload. |
| State which neighbours the ε protects | Production noise zS/N is calibrated for add/remove over a fixed public cohort. Read as replace-one, the same noise gives ε 18.1/8.6/2.0 in place of 8/4/1 (RDP, T = 20). With a cohort size that varies, even add/remove sensitivity reaches 2S/N ([adjacency analysis](production-dp-adjacency-analysis-2026-10-22.md)). | **Implemented locally:** `--dp-adjacency add-remove\|replace-one` (replace-one doubles the noise), `adjacency` on the eval card, and FedLoRA/DPFedAvg refuse a round whose cohort differs from `clients_per_round`. | The backend does not expose the choice yet, so platform runs are add/remove. |
| Calibrate DP noise exactly when every client takes part | With q = 1 the T conditional Gaussian guarantees compose to a μ-GDP bound, μ = √T/z. The RDP-calibrated z for ε 8/4/1 (T = 20, δ 10⁻⁵) actually gives ε 6.76/3.25/0.74. Exact calibration needs 13/17/24% less noise for the same claim. The closed form matches direct numerical integration to 10⁻⁶. | **Implemented locally:** `fedlearn.privacy.gdp_accountant`, `--dp-accountant gdp` (refused unless q = 1), `accountant` on the eval card, `plan_central_dp(accountant="gdp")`. **Platform default:** every backend-spawned DP run passes `--dp-accountant gdp` (live runs are always q = 1). The CLI default stays RDP. | Measured in [A13](effusion-gdp-calibration-r0-v1-result-2026-10-22.md): +0.017 AUROC at ε 4 and +0.018 at ε 1 for the same claim (intervals > 0). Exact only for full participation. |
| Zero-initialise a fresh linear head for DP training | Under a binding clip the head barely leaves its random init (weights move 0.24 against an init norm of 0.81), so the random direction caps AUROC. Zero init adds +0.019 at ε 4 and +0.028 at ε 8 at T = 20, and +0.15 to +0.20 at T = 5 ([A17](effusion-zero-head-r0-v1-result-2026-10-22.md)). With zero init, T = 5 has similar point estimates to T = 20 at ε 4–8 and a higher point at ε 1 (exploratory, not equivalence). | **Implemented locally:** `fl_server.py --head-init zero` zeroes the output layer of a FROZEN_HEAD/OVA_LP head (refused for FULL and for continued runs), and the eval card records `head_init`. Recommended under DP. **Platform default:** the backend passes it for every DP run on FROZEN_HEAD/OVA_LP, except continued runs. The CLI default is unchanged. | One workload, linear head. Zeroing only the output layer of an MLP head (e.g. PNEUMONIA_CNN) is standard practice but unmeasured here. The recipe was tuned for random init, and the T = 5 advantage is exploratory. No per-project switch in the UI. |
| Retain R0 as a conservative current default | A22 found a selected DINOv2 gate gain of +0.013 with a fragile lower bound +0.0007. Its private AUROC gains and DP-cost differences remain uncertain under separate patient/seed targets. | Keep R0 pending a fair representation/dimension comparison; DINOv2 remains a candidate. | Selection grid optimism, one workload, R0-tuned recipe; no equivalence or causal width-cancellation result. |
| Use the tested aggregation rankings as provisional baselines | A14 oracle stress tests found larger no-DP Fang damage for the mean than median. Under the DP-oriented recipe, mean outperformed the tested trimming variants. | Compare history-centered and other qualified rules before generalizing. Keep permitted-information private arms separate from nominally calibrated oracle stress cells. | Raw-honest oracle attacked cells do not have an established client-data epsilon; LR/clip differ across privacy recipes; one workload and older initialization/calibration. |
| Qualify label skew and attack damage separately | At alpha 0.1, A19 clean mean fell about 0.006 and private mean about 0.038. The additional privacy-cost increase is about 0.03253, seed-level 95% interval [0.01162,0.05343]. Under nonprivate Fang, median retained +0.129 over mean. | Include skewed clean and attacked controls in method qualification. | Simulated label/quantity skew, not site/scanner shift; alpha 1 is close on clean cells but has a larger Fang/mean decline. |
| Default recipes differ for clean and DP runs | Clean: LR 0.1, clip 1.0 gives 0.819, within 0.003 of the centralised probe ([A7](effusion-recipe-r0-v1-result-2026-10-22.md)). DP: LR 0.03, clip 0.05 gives 0.743/0.695 at ε 8/4. Reusing the clean clip under DP loses about 0.06 ([A8](effusion-dp-recipe-r0-v1-result-2026-10-22.md)). | Ship separate clean and DP defaults for frozen-feature heads. Never reuse a clean-tuned clip for DP. Fix DP hyperparameters in advance or account for tuning. | Two-seed selection; tuning privacy unaccounted; one workload. |
| The tested rule ordering depends on the privacy-oriented recipe | A9 found trimming reduced own-update sign-flip damage without DP, but had lower utility than mean at nominal epsilon4. | Retain mean/trimming controls; qualify newer rules and attacks before recommending a general defence. | One fraction and weak non-adaptive attacks; later oracle stress results have a different information boundary. |
| Measure secure aggregation arithmetic, bandwidth and trust separately | A5 matched plain aggregation within fixed-point error and reported 158x upload for the existing in-process implementation. | Show modeled/measured bytes and explicit trust assumptions. The current sum-only path does not implement robust nonlinear aggregation. | No network utility proof or general impossibility for other secure protocols; input validation remains a separate problem. |
| Distributed-noise simulation requires explicit unknown-honest-noise assumptions | A23 has close calibrated noise to central RDP at 16 bits, with unresolved five-seed utility differences. Four clip-skipping clients caused AUROC 0.25; modeled coded uploads are 4.4x plain. | Keep simulator-only status. Before deployment, qualify private entropy/transport, sufficient unknown honest noise, collusion/dropout behavior and input validation. | Current mu/N allocation does not establish DP against 40 colluders. Deterministic masks model a secure-sum interface; no operational confidentiality or utility equivalence is established. |
| Report internal ranking and transferred operating points separately | **Holdout**, one-time, 8,688 images: FedAvg 0.836 vs centralised 0.835 AUROC (+0.001 [−0.002, +0.004]). At a selection-frozen 0.90-sensitivity threshold, sensitivity held at about 0.91 for every model, while specificity fell from 0.55 (no DP) to 0.39/0.30/0.14 at ε 8/4/1 ([holdout](effusion-holdout-v1-result-2026-10-22.md)). | Present the measured specificity loss at the transferred thresholds beside AUROC; do not generalize it to every sensitivity setting. Freeze thresholds on development data and transfer them unchanged. | Internal holdout from the same NIH source; client-level ε; PPV 0.19 at best, so not clinical qualification. |
| Local DP had substantially weaker observed utility in this recipe | A11 local seed means at epsilon 1/4/8 were 0.525/0.540/0.557. At epsilon 8 its local-minus-central seed-level 95% interval is [-0.28388,-0.08806]. The epsilon 128 local point 0.742 is close to central epsilon 8 point 0.743. | Keep central DP as the tested trusted-server baseline; qualify other local recipes before offering defaults. | Different trust guarantees; substantial seed spread, no equivalence or general epsilon conversion, one recipe/cohort. |
| Treat local-DP norm and KS filters as evaluated partial defences | A20 filtered most noise-skipping attackers, but A21 norm-matching uploads evaded it and the KS filter left substantial damage. | Retain failures and explore joint/integrity controls before recommending protection. | Postprocessing of locally privatized uploads; marginal tests and norm proofs do not rule out all in-bound malicious directions. |
| The tested local-DP rules were fragile to noise-skipping attackers | A12 observed sign-flip AUROC around 0.30 at 20% attackers under mean, trimming and median. Its epsilon 8 mean attack-drop seed-level 95% interval remains positive. | Do not present those coordinate-wise rules alone as an effective defence for this recipe. | One fraction/protocol; neither a universal local-DP impossibility result nor proof that noise-aware filtering is sufficient. |
