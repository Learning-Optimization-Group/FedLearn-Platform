# Practical decisions for FedLearn

20 October 2026. The fixed NIH study completed all 360 cells and passed independent
model/score/accounting reconciliation. The [study report](nih-phase2-study-v1-report.md)
and retained tables support the conditional recommendations below. The additional
source-bound reader reproduced the full analysis exactly. Study review and its
validation correction are verified. The worker and persistent API now pass their
execution gates; the dashboard UI/browser evidence and final assessed deliverables
remain open.

## Decisions supported by the fixed study

The primary interval is conditional on fixed cohorts and ten simulation seeds.
Head/cohort/clipping results use three matched seeds and are descriptive. None is
a production privacy-budget choice, clinical recommendation or universal rule ranking.

| Decision | Measured evidence | Concrete platform action | Applicability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Choose aggregation from absolute utility and its control | At C0.05 / epsilon 4/sign-flip, mean AUROC 0.627389 versus trimming 0.618250. The primary interaction is nevertheless +0.002266, with paired 95% CI [+0.000704,+0.003828]. | Show clean/attacked absolute endpoints and each matched no-DP control beside the interaction and rule-specific sensitivity/noise. | Ten-seed linear-head primary setting; this does not establish a universal winner. |
| Qualify clipping jointly with attacks and DP | At C0.4/sign-flip, no-DP mean is 0.495854 versus trimming 0.658057; epsilon 4 values are 0.520096 and 0.546027. | Validate clipping with the intended threat/privacy recipe. Preserve the frozen study; new tuning belongs on validation in a separate experiment. | Three seeds; clipping changes useful signal, permitted attack magnitude and noise together. |
| Demand a learning benefit before increasing the head | Clean epsilon 4 mean: linear 0.644213 versus MLP512 0.643233; modeled upload 6.8 MB versus 1,688.0688 MB across 80 clients/20 rounds. | Put matched learning controls and payload estimates into head/model admission and comparison views. | About 248.245 times the payload; no equivalence test or claim that a differently tuned larger head cannot improve. |
| Qualify learning as cohort size changes | Clean epsilon 4 mean: 10 clients 0.548356, 80 clients 0.644213, 1,000 clients 0.630644. The thousand-client private/no-DP gap is smaller, but its private point mean is lower than at 80. | Report accepted cohort and local training recipe together; requalify local steps before scaling client count. | Synthetic patient groups and three seeds; not physical-device scaling or a pure 1/n noise experiment. |
| Treat threshold transfer as a measured operating point | For clean epsilon 4 mean, validation 80% recall transfers to test recall 0.915729 and specificity 0.241375; default test recall is 0.619790. | Display the validation target, transferred threshold and actual held-out confusion counts/recall/specificity. | Ten-model mean; no demonstrated clinical threshold or guarantee of 80% recall on new data. |
| Keep the evaluation unit visible | For that same group, inverse-patient weighting raises AUROC by 0.077657 on average, from 0.646336 to 0.723993. | Retain the original image endpoint and show patient-weight sensitivity alongside it. | A change of weighting, not a patient classifier; do not replace the prespecified endpoint after seeing the higher value. |
| Preserve the content-audit boundary | Removing the nine prespecified ambiguous images changes any model's AUROC by at most 0.000165502. | Keep the official primary cohort and report the fixed secondary mask, rather than remove inconvenient test cases. | Small measured sensitivity does not certify all labels, near-duplicates or clinical validity. |
| Expose actual execution and resource scopes | All 360 cells/7,200 rounds qualified; child 10446.316 s plus parent 2834.088 s. Maximum engine-completion high-water RSS 1573.469 MiB. | Show lifecycle, stage timings, accepted counts, completion/failure and measured-versus-modeled fields explicitly. | No process-tree/late-artifact memory cap or isolated hardware runtime comparison; power/background/fixed-order limitations apply. |

All comparisons refer to the pinned frozen-feature NIH workload. The mobile
pneumonia task and production image-training recipes require their own qualification.
The Simulation Lab should expose these comparisons and limitations. Its persistent
API has passed the execution gate; the React workflow and actual browser acceptance
remain open. The [follow-up readiness audit](privacy-security-readiness-2026-10-20.md)
separates central DP, generic local DP and DeComFL secure aggregation, with the
remaining qualification/reporting gates. It supplies no new disease-model result.

## Decisions supported by the Effusion follow-up (22 October)

All evidence below comes from the pre-registered
[Effusion protocol](effusion-baseline-protocol-v1.md) and its amendments A1–A10. The
setting is R0 frozen ImageNet features, a linear head, 80 whole-patient IID simulated
clients and client-level DP, evaluated on the selection half unless marked "holdout".
Platform changes are cited by their local commit; none are pushed.

| Decision | Measured evidence | Platform action | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A privacy flag must never be silently ignored | Before `5fad502`, a DP-enabled or regulated project on FedAvg, FedProx, FedOpt, Robust or DeComFL trained **without DP** and registered an artifact the gate accepted. Secure aggregation off DeComFL logged "ENABLED" while updates went in plaintext. | **Shipped (`5fad502`):** backend spawn seam and `fl_server.py` refuse DP on strategies without it and secure aggregation off DeComFL. Tests encode the refusal. | Reached through the API (`dpEnabled`/`regulated`); the dashboard exposes no DP control. Merge to the shipping branch is the user's decision. |
| FedAvg projects need real central DP | `DPFedAvg` reuses FedLoRA's mechanism and accountant. It is verified through the production coordinator and safetensors wire, and z = 0 on equal shards reproduces FedAvg exactly. | **Shipped (`969755a`):** `--strategy FedAvg --dp-enabled` builds `DPFedAvg`, with the eval-card DP trace and FedLoRA's live-accounting refusals. | Uniform (not example-weighted) averaging under DP. No physical-device run yet. |
| Plan ε before a run | The noise-norm-to-clip ratio is a hard lower bound on per-round noise-to-signal: z√d/N for add/remove noise zC/N (the planner and production `dp_aggregate`), 2z√d/N for replace-one 2C/N (the capstone simulator). At ε 4, d = 1,026 and N = 80 that is about 2.3 or 4.6 respectively (corrected 22 October; `plan_central_dp(adjacency="replace_one")` now gives the second figure). Head width cost: the 2× wider R1 head lost more AUROC at every ε (+0.007/+0.017/+0.051 at ε 8/4/1, all intervals > 0; [A4](effusion-privacy-secure-v1-result-2026-10-22.md)). | **Shipped (`969755a`):** `fedlearn.privacy.planner.plan_central_dp`. Prefer narrow trainable heads (frozen-head arms) for DP projects. | Formula-exact; the utility mapping comes from one workload. |
| State which neighbours the ε protects | Production noise zS/N is calibrated for add/remove over a fixed public cohort. Read as replace-one, the same noise gives ε 18.1/8.6/2.0 in place of 8/4/1 (RDP, T = 20). With a cohort size that varies, even add/remove sensitivity reaches 2S/N ([adjacency analysis](production-dp-adjacency-analysis-2026-10-22.md)). | **Shipped:** `--dp-adjacency add-remove\|replace-one` (replace-one doubles the noise), `adjacency` on the eval card, and FedLoRA/DPFedAvg refuse a round whose cohort differs from `clients_per_round`. | The backend does not expose the choice yet, so platform runs are add/remove. |
| Calibrate DP noise exactly when every client takes part | With q = 1 the T releases compose exactly to μ-GDP, μ = √T/z. The RDP-calibrated z for ε 8/4/1 (T = 20, δ 10⁻⁵) actually gives ε 6.76/3.25/0.74. Exact calibration needs 13/17/24% less noise for the same claim. The closed form matches direct numerical integration to 10⁻⁶. | **Shipped:** `fedlearn.privacy.gdp_accountant`, `--dp-accountant gdp` (refused unless q = 1), `accountant` on the eval card, `plan_central_dp(accountant="gdp")`. **Platform default:** every backend-spawned DP run passes `--dp-accountant gdp` (live runs are always q = 1). The CLI default stays RDP. | Measured in [A13](effusion-gdp-calibration-r0-v1-result-2026-10-22.md): +0.017 AUROC at ε 4 and +0.018 at ε 1 for the same claim (intervals > 0). Exact only for full participation. |
| Zero-initialise a fresh linear head for DP training | Under a binding clip the head barely leaves its random init (weights move 0.24 against an init norm of 0.81), so the random direction caps AUROC. Zero init adds +0.019 at ε 4 and +0.028 at ε 8 at T = 20, and +0.15 to +0.20 at T = 5 ([A17](effusion-zero-head-r0-v1-result-2026-10-22.md)). With zero init, T = 5 ties T = 20 at ε 4–8 and beats it at ε 1 (exploratory). | **Shipped:** `fl_server.py --head-init zero` zeroes the output layer of a FROZEN_HEAD/OVA_LP head (refused for FULL and for continued runs), and the eval card records `head_init`. Recommended under DP. **Platform default:** the backend passes it for every DP run on FROZEN_HEAD/OVA_LP, except continued runs. The CLI default is unchanged. | One workload, linear head. Zeroing only the output layer of an MLP head (e.g. PNEUMONIA_CNN) is standard practice but unmeasured here. The recipe was tuned for random init, and the T = 5 advantage is exploratory. No per-project switch in the UI. |
| Pick the aggregation rule by threat and privacy mode | Attacks built for robust rules ([A14](effusion-strong-attacks-r0-v1-result-2026-10-22.md)): without DP, Fang's attack at 20% costs the mean 0.155 and the median 0.063 AUROC; at 5% every rule loses at most 0.006. Under central DP every trimmed variant (k = 1, 4, 16) is below the mean in every condition, including Fang's attack. Small k buys no robustness. IPM 0.1 is harmless without DP but costs 0.053 under DP. | No DP with ≥10% possible attackers: median. Central DP: mean with a binding clip, no trimming, and limit or vet participation. | IID, one workload; attacks not adaptive to the clip; one ε. |
| Expect heterogeneity to raise DP cost and attack damage | Patient-whole Dirichlet label skew ([A19](effusion-noniid-r0-v1-result-2026-10-22.md)). At α 1.0 every result matches IID within about 0.004. At α 0.1 (29–41 of 80 clients with no positives): clean mean −0.006, median −0.018, central DP ε 4 −0.038. Under Fang's attack at 20%, the mean falls to chance (0.508) while the median keeps +0.129 over it. | Treat IID results as valid for moderately heterogeneous sites. For strongly skewed federations, budget for a larger DP cost and use the median when attackers are plausible and DP is off. | Label and quantity skew only (no feature or site shift); one workload. |
| Default recipes differ for clean and DP runs | Clean: LR 0.1, clip 1.0 gives 0.819, within 0.003 of the centralised probe ([A7](effusion-recipe-r0-v1-result-2026-10-22.md)). DP: LR 0.03, clip 0.05 gives 0.743/0.695 at ε 8/4. Reusing the clean clip under DP loses about 0.06 ([A8](effusion-dp-recipe-r0-v1-result-2026-10-22.md)). | Ship separate clean and DP defaults for frozen-feature heads. Never reuse a clean-tuned clip for DP. Fix DP hyperparameters in advance or account for tuning. | Two-seed selection; tuning privacy unaccounted; one workload. |
| Robust rule choice depends on DP | No DP: trimmed mean cuts sign-flip loss from 0.029 to 0.010 with no clean cost. Under ε 4 DP its 2C/(n − 2k) sensitivity makes it worse than mean in every condition ([A9](effusion-attacks-r0-v1-result-2026-10-22.md)). | Recommend `robust`/trimmed mean for non-DP projects facing poisoning risk. Keep refusing DP + Robust (already enforced). Pair DP projects with client authentication. | One attacker fraction; non-adaptive attacks; ALIE at its bound had no effect. |
| Secure aggregation is a bandwidth decision, not an accuracy one | LightSecAgg (existing implementation, in-process) matched plain aggregation within fixed-point error (AUROC −1e-7). It uploads 158× the bytes (1.32 MB vs 8.3 KB per client per round), and clients must self-clip ([A5](effusion-privacy-secure-v1-result-2026-10-22.md)). | Show the bandwidth and trust trade-off at configuration time. Secure + trimmed mean/median/Krum is unavailable by construction. | DeComFL-scalar path in production; head-path measurement in-process, no network. |
| Federation need not cost accuracy; DP costs specificity | **Holdout**, one-time, 8,688 images: FedAvg 0.836 vs centralised 0.835 AUROC (+0.001 [−0.002, +0.004]). At a selection-frozen 0.90-sensitivity threshold, sensitivity held at about 0.91 for every model, while specificity fell from 0.55 (no DP) to 0.39/0.30/0.14 at ε 8/4/1 ([holdout](effusion-holdout-v1-result-2026-10-22.md)). | Present DP cost to project owners as lost specificity (false alarms) at a fixed sensitivity, beside AUROC. Freeze thresholds on development data and transfer them unchanged. | Internal holdout from the same NIH source; client-level ε; PPV 0.19 at best, so not clinical qualification. |
| Local DP needs a much larger budget than central DP | Client-level local DP at ε 1/4/8 scores 0.525/0.540/0.557 (near chance), against 0.743 for central DP at ε 8. Local DP needs about ε 128 (0.742) to match central ε 8 ([A11](effusion-local-dp-r0-v1-result-2026-10-22.md)). | Do not add local DP as a default for small-cohort federations. Prefer central DP with a trusted aggregator. Any local-DP option should ship with planner warnings and this evidence. | 80 simulated clients; one recipe; larger cohorts narrow the gap (not measured). |
| Local DP is fragile against poisoning | With 20% non-compliant attackers, local DP at ε 8/32/128 lets sign flip drive AUROC to about 0.30 (below chance) under mean, trimmed mean and median alike. Robust rules do not help and the median is worse under label flip ([A12](effusion-local-robust-r0-v1-result-2026-10-22.md)). Honest noise forces a generous norm bound, which disables norm bounding. | Never pair local DP with coordinate-wise robust rules as a defence. Require noise-aware norm validation or verifiable client norm proofs before offering local DP. | One attacker fraction; attackers skip local noise; noise-aware filtering not yet tested. |
